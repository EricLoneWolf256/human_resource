# 17 — Phase 4 User Guide

## Purpose

This guide covers day-to-day use of the demonstration-ready Phase 4 release. All supplied accounts and records are synthetic. Do not enter real staff data.

## Sign in

1. Open `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Sign in with one of the demo accounts in `README.md`.
3. If the account requires a password change, complete the Change password dialog before continuing. Other API calls are blocked until the password is changed. Escape and Cancel are available only when the password change is voluntary.

## Navigation

The sidebar is permission-aware. It collapses to a menu button on narrow screens. Use **Skip to content** to jump directly to the main area. The active page is marked with `aria-current="page"` by React Router.

Available modules depend on the signed-in role:

- Employees, Organization, Documents, Leave, Requests, Contracts, Onboarding, Recruitment, Performance, Training and Career.
- Notifications for task and decision alerts.
- Audit trail for users with `audit.view`.
- Access control and Delegations for security administrators.
- Reports & analytics for users with `report.view`.
- Careers portal, which is public and does not require a session.

## Reports & analytics

Open **Reports & analytics** from the sidebar.

1. Choose a **From** and **To** date. The default range covers the last 365 days.
2. Optionally select or paste an organizational unit ID. Leaving it empty uses the whole access scope.
3. Select **Apply filters**.

The page shows:

- Workforce totals, status, employment type, unit, grade and academic/non-academic split.
- Leave, contract, HR request, onboarding, probation, appraisal, training, vacancy and application measures.
- Governance counts only when the account has the related access/audit permission. Values marked **Not authorized** are deliberately withheld.

Bars are a visual aid; every value is also present in an accessible table. Report rows and columns are scoped to the signed-in user's effective grants. Counts are aggregated and contain no employee, applicant or complaint identifiers.

Select **Export CSV** only if the account has `report.export`. The export includes generation time, report parameters and aggregate rows, and it creates an `report.exported` audit event. Users with `report.view` but not `report.export` can view the page but cannot download the file.

## Common workflows

- **Employee records:** HR users create and maintain records; employees can update limited self-service fields and submit correction requests.
- **Leave:** employees submit requests; authorized approvers decide them; approved requests update balances.
- **HR requests:** employees submit service requests; HR assigns, progress and closes them. Highly restricted complaints are hidden from unauthorized users.
- **Documents:** upload metadata and content are validated; authorized verifiers approve or reject documents; downloads remain access-controlled.
- **Recruitment:** HR publishes vacancies, candidates apply through `/careers`, panel members score only assigned applications, and offers require a documented recommendation.
- **Performance and training:** HR runs cycles and courses; employees complete self-reviews and training; authorized users finalize and record completion.
- **Career movement:** promotion, transfer and acting cases move through eligibility, committee and decision stages before an effective assignment is created.

## Notifications and reminders

Open **Notifications** to review in-app alerts and mark them as read. Notifications intentionally avoid sensitive narratives. Contract and probation reminders are generated by the reminder scan and visible to authorized users.

## Troubleshooting

- **403 on a page or action:** the server denied a required permission. Check the effective role assignments shown in the header or ask an access administrator.
- **403 on a report export:** the account has view access but not `report.export`.
- **Empty report:** the selected unit may be outside the access scope or the date range may exclude activity. The counts are intentionally scoped.
- **Session ends after status change:** deactivating or suspending an account revokes active sessions immediately.
- **Stale filters:** select **Apply filters** again to refresh the report.
