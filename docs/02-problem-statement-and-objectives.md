# 02 — Problem Statement and Objectives

## 1. Problem statement
Human resource processes can become difficult to manage when employee information, requests, approvals, contracts, leave balances, documents and performance records are kept in paper files or separate spreadsheets. Staff may have to follow up manually, records can be incomplete or duplicated, and managers may not have a timely picture of staffing. Sensitive information also needs stronger controls than shared folders or informal access.

The proposed University HR and Staff Management System provides one controlled platform for recording employee information and managing HR processes from recruitment to exit.

## 2. General objective
To design and develop a secure, centralized digital platform that improves the university's HR record management, staff services, workflow coordination, reporting and accountability.

## 3. Specific objectives
1. Create a centralized and searchable employee database.
2. Digitize recruitment, onboarding and employment-document workflows.
3. Enable staff self-service for profile information, leave, requests and documents.
4. Route approvals to responsible supervisors, HODs, deans, HR or executives according to configurable rules.
5. Track contracts, probation, leave, training, appraisals and career progression.
6. Provide dashboards and reports for authorized workforce planning and decisions.
7. Enforce role-based and scope-based access to confidential information.
8. Record significant system and HR actions in an audit trail.
9. Improve communication through notifications and reminders.
10. Support a consistent, traceable employee lifecycle from applicant to exit.

## 4. Expected benefits
- Less paper and manual follow-up.
- Faster retrieval of employee records and supporting documents.
- Better visibility of pending work and approval bottlenecks.
- More consistent HR processes.
- Reduced risk of unauthorized disclosure or unnoticed changes.
- Improved employee experience through self-service.
- More dependable reporting for planning.

## 5. Problem-to-solution mapping
| Problem | System response |
|---|---|
| Employee records are scattered | Central employee profile and secure document repository |
| Requests are followed up manually | Workflow status, assigned owner and notifications |
| Approvals are unclear or delayed | Configurable approval chains, deadlines and escalation |
| Staff cannot easily see their own information | Employee self-service portal |
| HR cannot quickly prepare workforce summaries | Permission-aware dashboards and reports |
| Confidential records may be exposed | RBAC, scopes, restricted record access and audit logging |
| Staff lifecycle events are missed | Contract/probation/renewal reminders and lifecycle workflows |
