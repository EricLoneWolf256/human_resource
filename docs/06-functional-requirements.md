# 06 — Functional Requirements
Priorities: **P1** core release, **P2** next release, **P3** future enhancement. Priorities are provisional.
## A. Accounts and access (P1)
- FR-001: Authenticate users and manage secure sessions.
- FR-002: Assign multiple roles and scoped organizational assignments.
- FR-003: Enforce granular permissions on API and UI actions.
- FR-004: Support account activation, suspension and deactivation.
- FR-005: Record security-sensitive access and changes.
## B. Employee records (P1)
- FR-010: Create unique staff records and staff numbers.
- FR-011: Maintain identity, contact, emergency contact, qualifications, employment and assignment data.
- FR-012: Maintain employment status and effective-dated history.
- FR-013: Search/filter employee directory according to permissions.
- FR-014: Track record completeness and verification status.
- FR-015: Allow employees to update approved self-service fields and submit other changes for review.
## C. Organization and positions (P1)
- FR-020: Configure organizational units and hierarchy.
- FR-021: Manage positions, job descriptions, grades and vacancies.
- FR-022: Assign heads, supervisors and employees with effective dates.
- FR-023: View an organizational chart and staff lists.
## D. Recruitment (P1/P2)
- FR-030: Create and approve recruitment requisitions.
- FR-031: Publish vacancy information to a careers portal.
- FR-032: Receive applications and attachments; issue application tracking reference.
- FR-033: Track screening, shortlist, interviews, panel assignments and scorecards.
- FR-034: Record references, selection recommendation, offers and candidate communication.
- FR-035: Restrict candidates and panel information to authorized recruitment users.
## E. Onboarding and probation (P1)
- FR-040: Create onboarding checklist from accepted offer.
- FR-041: Track documents, verification, contract, orientation, department and supervisor.
- FR-042: Track account/ID/assets requests and acknowledgements.
- FR-043: Track probation dates, reviews, outcomes and reminders.
## F. Leave and attendance (P1)
- FR-050: Configure leave types and policy rules.
- FR-051: Apply for leave, attach supporting evidence and track status.
- FR-052: Route approval to supervisor/HOD/HR according to policy.
- FR-053: Calculate balances, carryover and approved adjustments with history.
- FR-054: Prevent or flag overlapping leave and invalid dates.
- FR-055: Record attendance, official duty, absence and correction requests if attendance module is enabled.
## G. Requests and complaints (P1/P2)
- FR-060: Submit HR requests for letters, documents, data corrections, assistance and other categories.
- FR-061: Assign owner, priority, status, deadline and escalation.
- FR-062: Support confidential complaint categories with restricted access.
- FR-063: Track investigation notes, attachments, resolution and closure history according to access policy.
## H. Performance management (P2)
- FR-070: Configure appraisal cycles, forms, objectives and KPIs.
- FR-071: Support self-review, supervisor review, HOD review and HR validation where applicable.
- FR-072: Provide academic and non-academic appraisal templates.
- FR-073: Record feedback, ratings, development plans and historical outcomes.
## I. Training and career progression (P2)
- FR-080: Maintain training catalog and employee training history.
- FR-081: Submit, approve and record training requests and attendance/certificates.
- FR-082: Track skills, certificates and expiry dates.
- FR-083: Manage promotion, transfer, acting appointment and career progression cases.
## J. Contracts and documents (P1)
- FR-090: Maintain employment contracts, amendments, renewals and expiry dates.
- FR-091: Generate expiry and probation reminders.
- FR-092: Upload, categorize, verify, version and permission documents.
- FR-093: Record document expiry, download activity and retention status.
## K. Compensation (P3 or later)
- FR-100: Manage restricted compensation structures and payroll-related data if approved.
- FR-101: Provide controlled exports or integration to finance/payroll systems.
- FR-102: Maintain adjustment history and independent approval.
## L. Notifications, dashboards and reports (P1)
- FR-110: Create event-driven in-app notifications and reminders.
- FR-111: Show role-aware dashboards and action queues.
- FR-112: Produce scoped HR reports and allow authorized exports.
- FR-113: Display workflow history and task ownership.
## M. Exit and configuration (P2)
- FR-120: Manage resignation, retirement, termination and clearance.
- FR-121: Track handover, assets, access deactivation and exit documents.
- FR-122: Configure policies, templates, workflow rules and notification preferences.
## N. Audit (P1)
- FR-130: Record significant data changes, approvals, exports and privileged access.
- FR-131: Search audit records by user, action, record and date, within permission.
- FR-132: Prevent ordinary users from altering audit records.
